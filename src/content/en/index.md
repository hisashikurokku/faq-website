## 2. What do you do in CE?
CE focuses on building components such as processors, memory systems, embedded devices and networks while assuring that software can efficiently interact with the underlying hardware. Computer engineers work on everything from microchips and robotics to operating systems and high-performance computing, emphasizing performance, reliability and efficiency.

This major does not include fixing TVs or fridges.
